Manual AUX Management

Operational challenges, delays, and coordination drag across legacy contact centers

⚠️ The Operational Challenge

Manual AUX requests and approvals through emails and group chats cause delays and make it difficult to track agent availability. During storms or outages, supervisors must manually contact agents to arrange additional staffing, which slows down response times. Agents and supervisors also need quick access to information and guidance, but without an AI assistant like Ask Shankar, finding the right support can take extra time.

🚨 Impact: Slower coordination, reduced visibility, and increased manual effort.
